## บทพูด

## Prompt (English) — ใช้ใน Flow โหมดเฟรมเริ่มต้น พร้อมภาพเฟรมแรกของตอนนี้

```
Dark blue dusk, very low light, heavy mist. Continue from the start frame with the same people, faces, clothes and lighting.
0-1s: normal forest sounds.
1s: every sound stops at once. Total silence.
1-5s: Prang freezes, eyes wide, barely breathing. Far behind her, the tall thin dark silhouette between the trees stays completely still and blurred.
5-8s: Prang whispers in Thai, trembling: "ทำไม... เงียบไปหมดเลย"
Camera: static close-up on Prang, the silhouette stays out of focus. One continuous shot, no cuts.
Mood: frozen fear, cinematic horror.
Audio: silence after the first second, only soft breathing, no music. Speak Thai only.
No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text.
```
