## บทพูด

> (เสียงจากในป่า) "นัท... มาทางนี้" / (ต้นกระซิบ) "นั่น... ไม่ใช่เสียงเรา"

## Prompt (English) — คัดลอกทั้งกล่องไปวางใน Flow

```
Subject: Three Thai friends: (1) Nat, a 24-year-old Thai man, slim build, short messy black hair, light-tan skin, thin face, wearing a red-and-black flannel shirt open over a black T-shirt, olive-green cargo pants, brown hiking boots, a gray backpack, and a headlamp on his forehead; (2) Prang, a 23-year-old Thai woman, petite, shoulder-length straight black hair tied in a low ponytail, fair skin, round face, wearing a mustard-yellow rain jacket, black leggings, brown hiking boots, carrying a small silver flashlight; (3) Ton, a 26-year-old Thai man, stocky build, black buzz-cut hair, medium-brown skin, round black-framed glasses, wearing a navy-blue fleece jacket, gray cargo pants, black hiking boots, carrying a large orange tent bag on his shoulder
Setting: a dense tropical evergreen forest in the mountains of northern Thailand, towering trees with hanging vines, thick fern undergrowth, low drifting mist, at night, almost pitch black, lit only by Nat's headlamp and Prang's flashlight
Action: Nat and Prang face the darkness. From deep in the forest, a voice that sounds like Ton calls out. Nat takes a step toward it; Prang grabs his arm. The camera turns to reveal Ton standing right behind them, pale and terrified
Camera: Medium shot of Nat and Prang from the front, then a slow pan to reveal Ton behind them
Dialogue: An echoing off-screen voice that sounds like Ton but slightly wrong calls in Thai: "นัท... มาทางนี้". Then Ton, standing behind them, whispers in Thai: "นั่น... ไม่ใช่เสียงเรา"
Style: Cinematic horror film, realistic, muted desaturated greens, deep shadows, subtle film grain, shallow depth of field, 9:16 vertical. No subtitles, no on-screen text
Audio: Silent forest, the distant echoing voice, heavy breathing, no background music
```
