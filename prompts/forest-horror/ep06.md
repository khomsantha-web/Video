## บทพูด

```
Night horror scene, very dark. A headlamp and a small flashlight are the only lights; everything else is black. Continue from the start frame with the same three people, faces, clothes and lighting.
0-3s: from deep in the black forest, an echoing voice that sounds like Ton calls in Thai: "นัท... มาทางนี้"

5-8s: behind them, Ton steps into the light, pale and terrified, and whispers in Thai: "นั่น... ไม่ใช่เสียงเรา"
Camera: slow pan from Nat and Prang to Ton behind them. One continuous shot, no cuts.
Mood: terror, cinematic horror.
Audio: silent forest, the distant echoing voice, heavy breathing, no music. Speak Thai only.
No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text.
```
