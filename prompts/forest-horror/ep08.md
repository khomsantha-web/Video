# ป่าไม่มีทางออก — ตอนที่ 8: ถุงนอนใบที่สี่

- **ตัวละคร:** นัท, ปราง, ต้น (ดู [characters/](../../characters/))
- **ความยาว:** 8 วินาที
- **สัดส่วนภาพ:** 9:16 (แนวตั้ง)
- **ภาษาบทพูด:** ไทย

## บทพูด

> ถุงนอนใบที่สี่... ของใคร

## Prompt (English) — คัดลอกทั้งกล่องไปวางใน Flow

```
Subject: Three Thai friends: (1) Nat, a 24-year-old Thai man, slim build, short messy black hair, light-tan skin, thin face, wearing a red-and-black flannel shirt open over a black T-shirt, olive-green cargo pants, brown hiking boots, a gray backpack, and a headlamp on his forehead; (2) Prang, a 23-year-old Thai woman, petite, shoulder-length straight black hair tied in a low ponytail, fair skin, round face, wearing a mustard-yellow rain jacket, black leggings, brown hiking boots, carrying a small silver flashlight; (3) Ton, a 26-year-old Thai man, stocky build, black buzz-cut hair, medium-brown skin, round black-framed glasses, wearing a navy-blue fleece jacket, gray cargo pants, black hiking boots, carrying a large orange tent bag on his shoulder
Setting: a dense tropical evergreen forest in the mountains of northern Thailand, towering trees with hanging vines, thick fern undergrowth, low drifting mist, at night. A clearing with a burning campfire and an orange two-person tent: the same campsite from the first night, the same tent that Ton is still carrying packed in his bag
Action: The three run through the trees toward the glow of a fire and burst into the clearing. They stop. Beside the fire lie four sleeping bags in a row; the fourth is unzipped, as if someone just got out. Nat whispers. The fire suddenly goes out and everything goes black
Camera: Smooth tracking shot from behind as they run, opening into a wide reveal of the campsite, then a slow push-in on the fourth sleeping bag before cutting to black
Dialogue: Nat says in Thai: "ถุงนอนใบที่สี่... ของใคร", breathless whisper
Style: Cinematic horror film, realistic, muted desaturated greens, deep shadows, subtle film grain, shallow depth of field, 9:16 vertical. No subtitles, no on-screen text
Audio: Running footsteps and heavy breathing, crackling fire, then a sudden whoosh as the fire dies and total silence, no background music
```
